# Changelog

## 3.0.1 – 3.0.9

Bug-fix releases for 3.0.0, including fixes for Dualcast's Cancel Casting and readying prompt.

## 3.0.0

- Updated for the non-Denuvo build of the game.
- The Blue Mage job moved from Valmafra to an empty job slot (56, `0x38`). If you already have Alicia, update her job with a memory editor.
- Updated sprites. Fixed portraits.
- New reaction for Alicia: **Replenish MP** restores MP when it triggers after she takes damage.
- New support ability for Lavian: **Dualcast**.
- Cross-job learning: Alicia can learn Blue Magicks outside the Blue Mage job while it's set as her secondary skillset. The same goes for Summoners and Ramza, so with Mettle set as secondary he can learn Ultima even when he isn't a Gallant Knight.
- Fixed Mighty Guard bugs. It now replaces Tail Sweep instead of Thunder Breath, so it's a green Dragon Beastmaster skill.
- Goblin Punch, Drain Touch and Water Anima cast instantly.

## 2.0.0

- Added the Red Mage (Lavian). The mod is now *FFT Blue and Red Mages*.

## 1.0.0

- First release: the Blue Mage job for Alicia.
